# Opening Cinematic

`scripts/story/opening_cinematic.gd` (class `OpeningCinematic`) plays the speedboat-and-storm opening in its own `SubViewport`/`World3D` (it cannot touch `Main`, physics or the HUD), following the twelve scenes of `scripts/story/opening_timeline.gd`.

**API** (used by the beach sequence to chain into it):
- `OpeningCinematic.play(tree: SceneTree) -> OpeningCinematic` — static, adds itself to `tree.root`.
- `signal finished(skipped: bool)` — fires exactly once, whether the cinematic plays out, is skipped, or the node is freed early (freeing early does not itself emit it).
- `func skip() -> void`.
- `var is_done: bool`.
- `func advance(seconds: float) -> void` — the real clock; `_process` just calls it, so a test can step the whole timeline without a window or real time.

**Visual honesty:** the protagonist, the guard (not yet built — see the beach doc) and the boat are simple procedural low-poly stand-ins (capsules, boxes, a wedge hull, small coin discs for the treasure), not the rigged game character. Milestone 13 is the place to swap in the real `SkinnedVisual` player model and a proper boat asset.

**Timeline:** 12 scenes, ~50.5 s total (aerial, tracking, bag close-up, face close-up, clouds appear, storm builds, struggle at the wheel, waves hit, overwhelmed, thrown overboard, to black, ambience crossfade). Storm intensity (0 to 1) is held at 0 through the first four scenes and climbs from scene 5 to scene 10. The camera moves between each scene's framing along an arc that passes above the boat, so a transition between two close shots does not clip through the hull — this was a real bug found and fixed while building this (the first straight-line version produced a screen-filling close-up of the bow mid-transition).

**Lightning and audio:** a lightning scheduler flashes more often as the storm builds and plays `menu_thunder` (generated by `tools/gen_sfx.gd`) with no extra delay (the strike and its sound are simultaneous here, unlike the menu background's distant-thunder delay). A looping wind bed (`menu_wind.wav`) plays throughout and fades out as the final scene calls `AudioManager.set_region_ambience(&"outpost", 3.0)` to crossfade into the beach ambience.

**Headless:** the whole 3D build (`SubViewportContainer`, lights, meshes, particles, audio players) is skipped on a headless run; only the cheap UI overlay (black screen, flash overlay, skip button) and the timeline logic exist, so `tests/opening_cinematic_check.gd` can step the full 50 s timeline in a fraction of a second.

**Tests:** `tests/opening_cinematic_check.gd`, 15 checks — timeline data (12 unique scenes, 45 to 70 s total, storm intensity not falling from scene 5 to 9, no two consecutive scenes sharing a camera position), headless cheapness, `finished` firing exactly once on a full run and on a skip (including a second skip and an early free), and pause-state restoration.

**Screenshots:** a handful of frames were captured from the real renderer (not just logic) and looked at; the boat, protagonist stand-in, treasure bag and nav light render correctly, and the camera-arc fix removed the hull-clipping bug. Full shot-by-shot art direction (lighting balance per scene, wave visual quality, rain density tuning) was not reviewed in detail — this is a first functional pass, not a polished cut.

**Not done:** no voice acting or spoken lines (captions are not part of this cinematic — the beach sequence may add a short muttered line); the guard-and-workers event is built separately in the beach sequence, not here; the ocean is a flat-colored plane (no per-vertex wave shader) to keep this simple and Compatibility-renderer-safe.
