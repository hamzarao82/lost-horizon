# The Director and the fortress enemies (Milestone 9)

Status date: 2026-11-07. Legend: **Verified** = a test or screenshot that was actually run. Nothing here has been played by a person.

## What exists
- `scripts/ai/director.gd` (`class_name Director`, extends `Enemy`): the final boss and the Fortress Captain mini-boss, one class with two profiles (`director`, `captain`).
- Scenes `scenes/enemies/Director.tscn`, `FortressCaptain.tscn` (a soldier base with the script, like the Warden).
- Six `EnemyConfig` resources in `resources/enemies/`: `fortress_infantry`, `elite_security_soldier`, `heavy_assault_unit`, `tactical_commander`, `fortress_captain`, `director`. The four line troops use the existing ranged brain; the captain and the Director use the move machine in `director.gd`.
- Twelve `AttackData` files (`fortress_*`, `director_*`), four loot tables `iron_fortress_*`, two unique items (`fortress_badge`, `director_signet`). All generated by `tools/gen_director_data.gd` (edit numbers there and rerun).
- `EnemyConfig.spawn()` now stores the config on the enemy (`set_meta`), because a freed config silently dropped the unique reward (found by `fortress_enemies_check`).

## The Director's phases
| Phase | Health | Behaviour |
|---|---|---|
| 1 Tactical | 100% to 60% | stays 6 to 12 m away (cover points when the level has them, strafing otherwise); aimed volley of 3 shots, a sweeping line of 5; a close counter when the player is within 3 m (4.5 s cooldown) |
| 2 Aggressive | 60% to 25% | 12% faster, shorter gaps (never below the recovery window); adds the dash strike; a volley is followed straight away by a dash |
| 3 Final stand | below 25% | 25% faster; closes to 3.5 to 7 m; wide expanding ring (6 m, jump over it) and a crossing barrage of 8 shots; each followed by a 0.9 to 1.0 s opening |

Each phase change: invulnerable for 2.0 s, a harmless ring, a caption, then 2.0 s of grace before the next attack. A hit that crosses two thresholds plays both transitions in order. A move is never repeated twice in a row.

| Move | Telegraph | Damage | Recovery (punish window) |
|---|---|---:|---|

| sweep | 48 frames | 8 per shot | 48 frames |
| counter | 36 frames (0.60 s) | 12 | 36 frames |

| ring | 54 frames (0.90 s) | 14, once, none if airborne | 54 frames |
| barrage | 60 frames (1.00 s) | 8 per shot | 60 frames |
Projectiles share the existing cap of 12 live shots; no move needs more than 8. Each shot hits the player at most once.

## Lines (captions through the existing path)
Phase 2: "You were never meant to find this place." Phase 3: "Every record burns. Every witness is a loose end. You are the last one." Defeat: "The Director falls."

## Defeat and persistence
Defeat clears rings and projectiles, plays the Warden-style hit-stop, drops the loot table's guaranteed items plus one roll, and sets the flag `iron_fortress_director_defeated` through `WorldState` exactly once (the flag is 32 characters; WorldState drops saved flags that are not `[a-z0-9_]` or longer than 48). `defeated_director` is emitted once. The signet is claimed once through the inventory's unique rule. A captain sets `iron_fortress_captain_defeated` instead.
**Not decided here:** what the region does with a saved defeat flag on load (it must not respawn the Director); that belongs to the Iron Fortress region builder.

## Honest gaps
- No new animation clips and no new model: the Director and the captain are the soldier model with a tint (dark, then redder in later phases) and a larger body. Attacks are shown by floor markers (line, ring), the existing windup pose and the weapon swing, not by custom poses. The phase 1 tint looked salmon in a screenshot because of the windup tint.
- Nobody has played the fight: difficulty, pacing and whether 600 health feels right are unjudged.
- Cover points are used if the level places them; none exist in any Iron Fortress scene yet.
- No sounds were added: attacks reuse existing sounds.
- The captain's move set is the same machine with other data; its charge is the dash.
- The HUD boss bar shows only health, with no phase markers.
- Web and Android were not run.
