## Enemies: fallbacks until the combat configs land
Placement uses the exact names `fortress_infantry`, `elite_security_soldier`, `heavy_assault_unit`, `tactical_commander`, `fortress_captain` and `director`. `FortressEnemies` loads `resources/enemies/<name>.tres` and, when the file does not exist, falls back to the closest Research Complex configuration (security guard, heavy guard, commander, elite subject). **In this branch all six are on fallbacks** (`FortressEnemies.missing()` lists them), so the fights are tested but are not the final fights. A config may be a `FacilityEnemyConfig` or an `EnemyConfig`; `FortressSpawn` handles both.

## Story and continuity
Seventeen entries (terminals and documents). They answer: who controlled the operation (the Director, Brigadier R. Kessel), why the experiments ran (a unit that does not tire, break or refuse), what the containment failure really was (a live field test the command ordered, reported as an accident), what the organization intended (an island garrison that cannot be turned, and a program to sell), why the protagonist is involved (the signal they answered was bait and a test), and what happens to the operation (stand-down order). Open threads left for optional future content: Manifest 9 left the island by sea and its buyer is unknown.

**Contradictions found and how they were resolved:**
1. The Research Complex exit document said "Access to the Iron Fortress road is sealed. For now." with a permanent sealed sign. The road now opens when the Black Site data is recovered, so the document and the tunnel sign were changed (the caption shows only until `research_complex_region_complete` is set).
2. Earlier regions name the Director by title only; this region adds the name and rank. It does not contradict them.
3. Outpost 17 says the signal was a distress call; the fortress now says it was bait. Treated as a reveal, not a contradiction: the Outpost 17 text never says the call was genuine.

## Known gaps

- Art is flat boxes and glow strips like the other regions; nobody has played the region, so balance and pacing are unjudged.
