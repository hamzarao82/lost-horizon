# Dead Coast (Milestone 7, region content)

Status date: 2026-11-06. Legend: **Implemented**, **Verified** (a test or screenshot was run), **Not tested**, **Not implemented**.

The Dead Coast is built as a self-contained content scene (`scenes/regions/DeadCoast_content.tscn`, root script `scripts/regions/dead_coast/dead_coast.gd`). It does not depend on the region framework being finished: it exposes what the framework needs and works standalone. **It is not yet reachable in the game**, because no region manager connects Outpost 17 to it.

## Layout
One strip following a bending shoreline, expressed as (d, z): d metres inland of the shore, z world north (negative). A bay pushes the shore east around z -168, which makes Smuggler's Cove.

| Area | Where | What is there |
|---|---|---|
| Wreckage Beach | z 20 to -45 | a freighter broken on the sand (breach in its inland wall, radio, data device), a lifeboat, crates, a beach patrol of two soldiers |
| Coastal Trail | z -45 to -100 | dock crane, truck wreck, two road blocks, an optional raider camp (two soldiers, a stash) |
| Abandoned Checkpoint | z -100 to -142 | booth with the security terminal and posted orders, guardhouse with the guard pass locker and a locker cache, watchtower, the barrier gate spanning the whole corridor, three defenders (heavy, soldier, elite) |
| Smuggler's Cove | z -142 to -195 | the bay, two pier sections, a second crane, two hulls, a smugglers' shack (consignment sheet), three hidden containers, a sealed equipment case, two guards |
| Cliffside Watchpoint | plateau d 38..68, z -172..-132, 10 m up | lighthouse stub, radio mast, sandbag nest, journal, locker cache, an elite guard; reached by a ramp that starts just beyond the gate |
| Hidden Supply Depot | pocket behind the cliff | reached by a cleft between two rocks near the cove; containers, generator, two caches, a vault with the unique reward, two guards |
| Exit toward Blackwood | z -195 to -222 | fence, sign, last barricade; locked until Lost Cargo is complete |

Terrain: a height field (sand, rock cliffs, a plateau, a ramp, a carved pocket and cleft) meshed at 4 m with vertex colours and a trimesh collider; a teal sea plane; a collision ribbon at the shoreline so the sea cannot be waded. Vegetation and rocks are six MultiMeshes with a visibility range.

## Missions (existing quest system, `resources/quests/dead_coast/`)
| Mission | Required objectives | Optional |
|---|---|---|
| Wreckage (`dc_wreckage`) | enter the beach; inspect the hull; search the ship radio; find the cargo manifest; defeat the beach patrol; recover the data device | search the lifeboat cache |
| The Silent Checkpoint (`dc_silent_checkpoint`) | locate the checkpoint; read the security terminal; read the posted orders; obtain the guard pass; defeat the defenders | search the guardhouse |
| Lost Cargo (`dc_lost_cargo`) | follow the drag marks; enter the cove; open three hidden containers; recover the equipment case; read the consignment sheet | reach the watchpoint, find the depot, clear the raider camp |

The three chain through `next_quest`. Objectives are event driven, so they can be done in any order and objectives done before a mission starts are remembered (tested). Rewards (items granted once by the quest manager): upgrade kit and credits; armor patch and credits; the unique Coastal Weapon Part and credits. XP is the default quest XP of the progression config. The unique accessory Tide Charm is in the depot vault.

## Persistence
Every persistent id begins with `dead_coast.`: crates become `<id>_taken`, documents `doc_<id>_found`, patrol groups `patrol_<id>_cleared` (a cleared squad never respawns), all saved with the world flags. `capture_state()` and `restore_state()` give the region framework a per-region dictionary (claimed, read, cleared, flags) and are idempotent; hostile input is ignored.

## Interfaces for the framework
`entry_points` (`beach_arrival`, `from_blackwood`), `exit_points` (`to_outpost_17`, `to_blackwood` locked by `dead_coast.cargo_complete`), `enter(entry_id)` (starts the first mission once), metadata `region_id`, `ambience_name = "dead_coast"`, `environment_preset = "dead_coast"`, `vegetation_density`, `owns_lighting` (standalone sun and haze). The ambience and environment preset names are not consumed by anything yet and no new audio exists.

## Tests
`tests/dead_coast_check.gd` (69 checks: builder, a physics probe walking the whole route, ramp and cleft, the closed gate stopping the probe and not being bypassable, keep-clear zones, interactables not buried, patrols and the four-patroller cap, persistence, draw-call budget of 79) and `tests/dead_coast_missions_check.gd` (60 checks: three missions in order, out of order and across a save round trip, rewards once, optional objectives optional). The whole suite (50 suites) passed with zero script errors after the icon fix. Data is generated by `tools/gen_dead_coast_data.gd`.

## Honest limits
- Not reachable in the game yet (needs the region framework and a travel trigger).
- Art: stylized low-poly using the existing kit, three new props of Milestone 6 and procedural geometry. The beach is bright, the sea strongly saturated, the freighter and booth are boxes. It does not approach the reference images.
- The four-patroller cap means a group may wait for another to die before it spawns; a retry timer handles it and the cap is tested, but the pacing was not played.
- No human has played any of it; combat balance, encounter pacing and navigation readability are unjudged.
- Region-specific audio does not exist; the location banner for the coast areas is not wired into the HUD. The three new items use generated icons (`tools/gen_icons.gd`).
- Web and Android were not run.
