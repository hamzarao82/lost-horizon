# Teleport

Press **T** (gamepad **Back/Select**, touch **Tele** button) to jump to a random safe spot in the arena.

## Charges and recharge
- **2 charges** maximum.
- Charges come back **one at a time**, each **10 minutes** (`TeleportAbility.DEFAULT_RECHARGE_SECONDS = 600`) of real wall-clock time after the previous one.
  - Use one from full: the 10-minute timer starts.
  - Use the second while the timer runs: the timer keeps running (it is not restarted).
  - Both used at t = 0: first charge back at 10:00, second at 20:00.
  - Using one at 4:00 into a running timer: that charge is back at 10:00, the other at 20:00.
- The recharge uses the system clock and **keeps counting while the game is closed**. On start-up it catches up on every charge that should have returned.
- State is stored in `user://teleport.cfg` (`charges`, `ready_at` as unix time). **Reloading the scene (death restart) does not refund charges**, and neither does restarting the game.
- Damaged files cost a wait, not free charges:
  - A timer further than 10 minutes in the future (corrupt, or clock set back) is pulled back to now + 10 minutes.
  - Below the maximum with a missing, non-numeric or NaN timer: a full 10-minute wait.
  - Counts are clamped to 0..2. A missing or unreadable file starts full (same as a first launch).
- Headless runs (tests) use `user://teleport_test_<pid>.cfg` and delete it on exit.

## Where you land
- Inside the arena (|x|, |z| <= 26) at y = 0.1.
- Not within 4.5 m of the cargo containers at (8, -6) and (-8, -4).
- At least 8 m from where you were standing, and at least 6 m from every living enemy.
- Free of colliders (a physics query with the player's capsule on the world and enemy layers).
- Up to 20 strict tries; then the rules relax and the best candidate seen wins (best = farthest from trouble). If nothing was valid at all, the arena corner farthest from you is used.

## What happens
- You are invulnerable for 0.5 s (`Player.grant_invulnerability`) and your velocity is cleared. Lock-on target, stamina and health are untouched; an attack in progress is not cancelled.
- Works in every player state except dead (including staggered and mid-dodge).
- Effects: cyan burst plus an expanding ring at both ends and a short screen flash (`scripts/fx/teleport_fx.gd`, CPU particles, scaled by `Fx.quality_scale`), and the `teleport` sound (generated by `tools/gen_sfx.gd`).

## UI
Own label below the stamina bar (x = 16, or x = 84 when touch controls are on, to clear the pause button): `Teleport 2/2 [T]`, or `Teleport 1/2 [T]  next in 09:41`. It refreshes once per second and dims at 0/2. Pressing with no charge shakes the label and shows `Teleport recharging mm:ss`.

## Code
- `scripts/player/teleport.gd` - `TeleportAbility` (logic, persistence, UI).
- `scripts/player/teleport_watcher.gd` - autoload `Teleport`; adds an ability to every Player (no change to `player.gd` or `Player.tscn` is needed).
- `scripts/fx/teleport_fx.gd` - effects.
- Tests: `tests/teleport_check.gd` (headless). Inject a fake `clock` Callable and a short `recharge_seconds` to test timing.
