# Weapon and armor art (Milestone 002)

Original, procedurally generated low-poly models. No third-party assets, so there is nothing to attribute
(licence: the repository's MIT licence; generated by the scripts below).

## Files
| File | Purpose |
|---|---|
| `tools/blender/props_lib.py` | Shared Blender helpers (also used by the environment kit): `new_scene`, `box`, `cylinder`, `sphere`, `mesh_from`, `loft`, `bevel`, `paint`, `join`, `finalize`, `tri_count`, `export_glb` |
| `tools/blender/weapons_build.py` | Builds the 8 weapons into `assets/weapons/<item_id>.glb` |
| `tools/blender/weapons_armor.py` | Builds `assets/armor/warden_plate.glb` |

Rebuild everything:
```
"C:\Program Files\Blender Foundation\Blender 4.5\blender.exe" --background --python tools/blender/weapons_build.py
"C:\Program Files\Blender Foundation\Blender 4.5\blender.exe" --background --python tools/blender/weapons_armor.py
Godot --headless --path . --import
Godot --headless --path . -s tools/blender/verify_weapons.gd
```

## Contract (what the visual adapter can rely on)
- 1 unit = 1 metre. **Origin = centre of the grip.** The blade points toward **-Z** (Godot forward), up is +Y.
- **Orientation note:** the blade is a thin plate with its **width along X** (edges point sideways) and its
  **flat faces up/down (+-Y)**. This matches the existing code-built `WeaponModel` and the horizontal slashes of the
  swing pivot (which rotates about Y). The original brief said "flat facing +-X", which would put the edge on top
  and look wrong in a horizontal swing; if the hand animation needs the other roll, rotate the attach offset 90 degrees
  about Z.
- Single-edge blades (machete, katana, Warden's Cleaver) have the **edge on +X** and the spine on -X.
- One mesh, **one material, vertex colours only** (`COLOR_0`, linear). The Godot importer enables
  `vertex_color_use_as_albedo`; the verify script asserts it. Roughness 0.6, metallic 0.
- Rarity glow is applied by the engine (emission); only the Warden's Cleaver has baked crimson accents.
- Use `WeaponModel.scene_for(item_id)` to get the `PackedScene` (null when the file is missing, so callers fall back
  to the code-built model) and `WeaponModel.attach_offset()` for the local transform relative to the hand socket
  (identity: grip centre at the `Hand_R` BoneAttachment3D).

## Warden Plate (`assets/armor/warden_plate.glb`)
596 tris, 42 KB. Cuirass with crimson hem and neck trim, chest cross, gorget, two spiked pauldrons.
Origin at the Chest bone head (about 1.35 m above the feet), +Y up, chest front toward -Z, 0.72 m wide x 0.58 m tall x
0.28 m deep, centre about 0.12 m below the origin. Attach with a `BoneAttachment3D` on `Chest`.

## Known limits
- Stylized and blocky by design (flat shading, no textures); not the detail level of the milestone's concept art.
- The plate is an open shell: fine from outside, but if the character's torso is narrower than 0.34 m in x it will
  float, and wider than 0.46 m it will clip. Scale the attachment node if the final skeleton differs.
- Weapon models do not carry a trail or glow; those are separate effects.
